# Community Voice & Learning Intelligence

Clickable prototype for a conversation with CAMFED’s Evaluation and Learning team. **Synthetic data only.** Country A, three districts, 40 school codes, about 600 voices over 12 weeks. There are no real learner, school or CAMA names.

Altamont Advisory · prototype for discussion.

## Five-minute demo

1. **Pulse.** Start on the home view. Note voices heard, coverage by district, the share from girls and young women, and the share gathered by CAMA researchers. The theme lines run across 12 weeks. The district heatmap shows where themes concentrate.
2. **Early warning.** Read the District 2 card: cost-barrier mentions rise in weeks 9–12. Open **Act** and download the **Early Warning Alert** PDF.
3. **Validate a code.** Open **Validate**. Accept one suggestion, edit another, and add a context note. The agreement rate includes a prior review log plus this session. The point of the screen: a person confirms, the model only suggests.
4. **Generate a brief.** Still on **Act**, download the Community Voice Brief, the Programme Insight Brief or the Adaptive Learning Note.
5. **Action tracker.** Change a status. Each row keeps the insight, the decision, the owner and the due date.
6. **Safeguarding.** Open **Listen** and note that referred items are absent. Open **Safeguarding**. The learning-team view hides note text. Open the safeguarding lead workspace to see the note, the audit trail, and the consent log.

## Other screens

- **Analyse** clusters themes, shows confidence and quotes, and answers **Ask the evidence** only from this synthetic set, with voice IDs.
- **Gender lens** splits the same metrics by gender and age band.
- **Government** compares Learner Guide signals in government-led and CAMFED-led schools and exports a one-page evidence note.
- **How it fits** shows community channels → this layer → the CAMFED platform, dashboards and CRM, by CSV or API. It adds to those systems. It does not replace them.

Listening choices on Pulse carry into the charts, the feed, the clusters and the gender and government views.

## Data

Generated by `node scripts/generate-voice-data.mjs` into `src/data/voice-data.ts`. Language L is a constructed stand-in for a translation demo. It is not a real community language.

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Recharts, on TanStack Start (file routes, no login, no database). Synthetic evidence is precomputed in `src/data/voice-data.ts`, including codes, translations and draft briefs, so the demo runs without an external model. PDF briefs are generated in the browser.
